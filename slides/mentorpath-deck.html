The Problem

Kids take assessments.
Adults give advice.
Nobody shows them the path.

Guidance counselors are overwhelmed. Career tests say "you'd be a good architect" but don't answer "what does that actually look like?" or "what do I do tomorrow?" By the time most people figure it out, they've already pivoted three times.

MentorPath

The Solution

A mentor that lives with you
for years, not minutes

MentorPath is a virtual guidance counselor and mentor — powered by AI — that knows your goals, your interests, what you hate, and where you are in life. It doesn't teach. It gives direction.

Not a school Not a test A companion
MentorPath

How It Works

Three ways to find your path

01

AI Mentor

Chat with a warm, honest mentor who knows your profile. Ask anything: "What careers match me?" or "I'm scared of blood — can I still be a doctor?" It guides, it doesn't lecture.

02

Career Explorer

Browse real careers and see what the day-to-day actually looks like — salary, timeline, skills, and a "day in the life" snapshot. Virtual shadowing, zero awkwardness.

03

Journey Map

Pick a destination and your mentor maps every step from today to arrival — with milestones you check off, concrete actions, skills gained, and fun activities along the way.
